who art thou?

O great mountain? before Zerubabbel thou shalt become a plain: and he shall bring forth the headstone thereof with shoutings, crying, grace unto it.

I'm Emma, currently finishing up a B.A. at the University of Cape Town with on-paper majors in computer science and history, as well as a third non-official major in philosophy. I self-taught programming when I was fifteen, and since then it's taken up much of my time, but I force myself to grapple with the more traditional arts whenever I can.

On a hobby-project level I'd describe myself as pathologically obsessed with reinventing the wheel as a form of personal development, but I still like to believe I'm good at being pragmatic in serious environments. I find myself particularly fascinated with low-level systems like libc and browser DOMs both in and of themselves and with the ways we abstract over them.

My more informal writing, in which I am always wrong and missing important details, lives on a subdomain.

what crafts dost thou practise?

Nailing myself down to a specific subset of computer science has always been tricky. To average it out over a long period, most of the work I've done has been:

  • web — HTML/CSS/JS/TS/SCSS/ SPA/SSR/CSR/SSG/WOATMUSMTS.1
  • frameworks — Svelte mostly, some Next and Nuxt, a bit with Lit, Solid, and other oddballs.
  • app — good in Rust, passable in C and Go, questionable Zig knowledge.
  • design — competent in LaTeX, Typst, Affinity, Figma, and a few other bits and bobs.
Overall, I've found myself able to learn whatever I put my mind to, but these are the things I've had enough experience with to put here. I've also toyed many other languages, some game and engine development, and a bunch of other little things.
