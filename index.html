I'm Emma, currently finishing up a B.A. at the University of Cape Town with on-paper majors in computer science and history, as well as a third non-official major in philosophy. I self-taught programming when I was fifteen, and since then it's taken up much of my time, but I force myself to grapple with the more traditional arts whenever I can.

On a hobby-project level I'd describe myself as pathologically obsessed with reinventing the wheel as a form of personal development, but I still like to believe I'm good at being pragmatic in serious environments. I find myself particularly fascinated with low-level systems like libc and browser DOMs both in and of themselves and with the ways we abstract over them.

My more informal writing, in which I am always wrong and missing important details, lives on a subdomain.
